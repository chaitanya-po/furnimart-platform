# FurniMart Platform

Enterprise-grade omnichannel furniture retail platform.

## Business Overview

FurniMart is a furniture retail platform supporting:

- Online shopping
- Physical showroom inventory
- Product catalog
- Customer accounts
- Shopping cart
- Orders
- Payments
- Delivery
- Notifications
- Inventory management
- Sales analytics

## Architecture

The platform will evolve from a modular application into a
distributed microservices architecture.
